How this works

  1. Enter the four-character code you were sent. It is yours alone, so don't share it.
  2. Turn over your three cards: a drug, a NAM (new approach methodology) and an animal model. Your drug card decides your group.
  3. Research your cards. You may swap once, on either the NAM or the animal model (never the drug). You can come back to swap later; your hand will not change.
  4. Write down or screenshot your result code and bring it to the practical. If you swapped, be ready to explain why.
